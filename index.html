Hi, I'm Redwan Ahmed Rizvee.

PhD Student in CS @ UMass Amherst

I study how to make massive datasets interpretable in real-time. As a researcher at the DREAM Lab, advised by Dr. Alexandra Meliou, I develop Perception-Aware Sampling algorithms that bridge statistical learning theory with visual analytics. You can have a demo of our work in the PAWS Playground!

Previously, I was a Research Scientist at TigerIT and a Lecturer at the University of Dhaka. I am interested in Data Mining, Database Theory, and building scalable systems.

I also help build COLAS, a coding lab assistant that brings together automated evaluation, plagiarism analysis, and actionable feedback for students.

COLAS

Coding Lab Assistant

One of the lead architects & developers

Learning to program takes more than an accepted or wrong-answer verdict. COLAS brings code execution and grading, plagiarism detection, and AI-assisted feedback into one platform for programming labs.

My contribution. I help design and develop the system across its React interface, Flask backend, and MongoDB data layer, including evaluation workflows, code similarity analysis, and feedback that helps students understand mistakes and improve their solutions.
